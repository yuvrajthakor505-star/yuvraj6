Expert Advice

Beauty & Hair Care Secrets

Pro tips from Sharma Salon stylists to keep your hair glowing and healthy between salon visits.

Use sulfate-free & paraben-free shampoos, avoid tying hair tightly for 72 hours post-treatment, and apply heat protection serums before styling.
Wash hair with lukewarm or cold water, use a purple or color-protecting conditioner once a week, and minimize direct sunlight exposure.
We recommend visiting 2 to 3 weeks prior to your wedding day for skin preparation, hair trials, and facial consultations.
