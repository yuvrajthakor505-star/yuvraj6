Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our hair coloring, keratin treatments, and salon protocols.

We exclusively use ammonia-free, hair-safe international products from L'Oréal Professionnel, Schwarzkopf, and Olaplex bond repair builders to ensure zero damage and long-lasting vibrant gloss.
Our Keratin and Hair Botox treatments typically last between 3 to 5 months depending on your home care routine and use of recommended sulfate-free shampoos.
We offer complimentary face-to-face consultations prior to every major chemical service to assess your scalp health, hair elasticity, skin undertone, and desired outcome.
All our scissors, combs, and metal implements are UV-sanitized before every client. We use fresh disposable capes and maintain continuous air filtration for a pristine environment.
